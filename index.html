What is

My Pixel Kafe?

My Pixel Kafe is a simulation game where the player takes the role of a barista, preparing coffee orders for customers while maintaining a high reputation score.

How to Play

Enjoy the grind and brew your way to the top in Pixel Cafe!

1

Take The Order

Watch for customer bubbles and note their requests. Be quick—happy customers leave the best tips!

2

Brew the Blend

Tap the correct recipe to create the correct drink. Precision is key to making the perfect cup.

3

Collect Rewards

Earn coins and build your reputation with every successful serve. Use your riches to expand the shop!

4

Level Up Menu

Meet your daily requirements to unlock premium drink recipes and become a master of the menu.
